ZAEVI / ACCESSIBILITY

Accessibility.

We want everyone to be able to browse and shop at ZAEVI. We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. Last updated 3 November 2026.

What we have built in
A skip link and visible focus states for keyboard users; labelled form fields and navigation landmarks; text colours checked against contrast requirements; and pages that adapt to phones, tablets and desktops.
Motion and 3D effects
If your device is set to reduce motion, we turn off the moving 3D silk, the animations and the custom cursor. If your browser cannot show 3D graphics, the website uses a photograph instead. All 3D content is decorative or repeats information found elsewhere on the page.
Known limitations
The 3D Studio view relies on dragging or touch gestures, and its product details are also shown on the product page. Some product photographs are still being improved. This website has not yet had an independent accessibility audit.
