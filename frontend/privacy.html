ZAEVI / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

This policy explains what personal information ZAEVI collects through this website and how it is used. Last updated 3 November 2026.

Information you give us
Your name, email address, phone number and password when you create an account (passwords are stored only as a salted hash); delivery addresses; order details; reviews you submit; your email address if you subscribe to the newsletter; and anything you send through the contact form.
Information stored on your device
Your bag, wishlist and checkout draft are kept in your browser's local storage. We use a session cookie to keep you signed in and a security cookie that protects your account against forged requests. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics.
How we use your information
To make and deliver your order, provide order updates and tracking, run your account, answer your messages, handle returns and refunds, send the newsletter if you subscribed, and keep the website secure.
Who we share it with
Only those who help us run the service: our delivery carriers, our email provider and, when you pay online, our payment processor Razorpay, which handles your card or UPI details directly. We never receive or store your full payment details, and we do not sell your personal information.
Content from other services
Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and some photographs from Unsplash, so those services can see your IP address when your browser requests them.
How long we keep it
Order records are kept for as long as needed for delivery, returns and our legal and accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete your account and other information that we are not required to keep.
Your choices and rights
You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, and you can withdraw your consent, including by unsubscribing from the newsletter. Contact Client Services or .
Security
Access to customer information is restricted to authorised administrators. No website can guarantee absolute security, so please use a unique password and tell us if you suspect any misuse of your account.
Children
This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
Changes to this policy
We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
