The New Silhouette

THE JOURNAL

Silhouette is the first thing we notice about a garment, often before colour or fabric. It is the outline a piece draws around the body, and it changes how that body moves through a room.

A silhouette that feels new rarely comes from adding more. More often it comes from easing a line: a waist that wraps instead of grips, a skirt that opens with each step, a sleeve with room to move.

The result should feel effortless at a glance and considered up close. Movement becomes part of the design, and the garment looks different walking than it does standing still.
