CLIENT SERVICES

Frequently Asked.

Answers to inquiries regarding atelier craftsmanship, made-to-order timelines, fitting, and white-glove delivery. For tailored consultation, our concierge remains at your service.

How long does made-to-order delivery take?

Because every ZAEVI garment is individually cut and assembled in our atelier, production takes 5–7 business days, followed by 3–5 days for insured express delivery. You can monitor each stage live on our Track Order portal.

What is the return and exchange policy?

We offer 14 calendar days of complimentary returns and size exchanges from documented courier receipt. Pieces must remain unworn, unwashed, and in pristine condition with all House tags attached. See Returns & Exchanges for full guidelines.

How do I select the perfect size or request bespoke fit?

Consult our detailed Size Guide with unit toggles between centimeters and inches. You may also add personalized fit notes directly at checkout, or contact our concierge for dedicated measurement guidance.

How can I track my order in real-time?

Enter your order reference (e.g. ZV-20261020-…) and checkout email on our Track Order page. Our atelier status tracks Order Received, Confirmed, In Production, Quality Check, Packed, and Dispatched.

How should I care for my ZAEVI garments?

Always consult the hand-sewn interior care label. Store bespoke tailoring on contoured wooden hangers and knitwear folded flat in cool, dry settings. We advise professional eco-friendly dry cleaning for silk, wool, and structured outerwear.

Do you ship worldwide with taxes and duties handled?

Yes. International orders are dispatched via premium bonded carriers with all customs duties and taxes included at checkout for supported global destinations. No surprise customs fees upon arrival.

Are ZAEVI pieces mass produced or limited edition?

ZAEVI rejects fast-fashion mass production. Every creation is produced on-demand in limited bespoke runs to eliminate fabric waste and ensure the highest standards of artisan construction.
